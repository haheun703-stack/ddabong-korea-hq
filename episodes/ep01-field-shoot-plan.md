# DDABONG KOREA — EP01 Gyeongju Field Shooting Plan

Date: 2026-08-16
Episode: Gyeongju Royal Tombs

## Decision
For the present-day layer of EP01, original footage is the preferred source.

We should personally capture modern Gyeongju, Daereungwon, Cheonmachong exterior/visitor experience, streets around the tombs, people-scale comparisons, and modern-life-versus-ancient-tomb contrasts. This gives DDABONG KOREA a real documentary layer and reduces rights problems.

## Current official visitor baseline
- Gyeongju official tourism pages describe Daereungwon as a downtown tomb park with 23 Silla-period tombs.
- Current 2026 tourism pages list Daereungwon as free admission and Cheonmachong as paid admission.
- Current tourism information lists the Daereungwon/Cheonmachong viewing window around 09:00–22:00; confirm on the actual shooting day because operating conditions can change.
- Gyeongju tourism itself recommends magic-hour photography for Daereungwon.

## SHOOT DAY — MUST HAVE

### 1. Opening: “modern city + giant tombs”
Purpose: first 20 seconds.

1. Street-level shot of normal Gyeongju traffic/pedestrians.
2. Café/shop foreground with mound visible in background.
3. Slow walk/gimbal reveal from building/wall/tree to giant mound.
4. Static wide with modern street and mound in same frame.
5. Person crossing frame to show mound scale.
6. Close foreground detail — sign, bicycle, coffee cup, crosswalk — rack/tilt toward mound.

Target: 6–10 clips, each 8–15 seconds.

### 2. Daereungwon: scale and landscape
Purpose: establish that this is not “one grave.”

1. Wide mound cluster.
2. Path between multiple mounds.
3. Low-angle mound against sky.
4. Long-lens layered mounds.
5. Visitors walking between mounds.
6. Child/adult silhouette for scale.
7. Trees + mound composition.
8. Empty path ambience.
9. Golden-hour side light on mound.
10. Evening/night entrance or path if useful.

Target: 12–15 clips.

### 3. Cheonmachong: case-study footage
Purpose: 1:25–2:15 section.

1. Cheonmachong name/sign.
2. Exterior mound.
3. Entrance approach.
4. Visitors entering/leaving.
5. Entrance detail.
6. If interior photography/video is permitted on site: neutral visitor-perspective details only.
7. Do not assume commercial filming permission inside; if using more than ordinary personal-style handheld capture, ask staff before shooting.

Target: 6–8 exterior clips minimum.

### 4. “The city lives around it”
Purpose: 6:05–closing.

1. Café/restaurant street near tomb area.
2. Cars/buses moving with mound in frame.
3. People taking photos.
4. Couple/family walking beside tombs.
5. Modern signage + mound.
6. Smartphone screen photographing mound.
7. Slow evening pedestrian shot.
8. Final sunset/twilight wide.

Target: 8–12 clips.

### 5. Texture / edit-rescue B-roll
Purpose: transitions and narration coverage.

1. Grass moving in wind.
2. Footsteps on path.
3. Tree leaves against mound.
4. Mound edge detail.
5. Hands holding brochure/map.

Target: 10–15 clips.

## Camera rules for DDABONG
- Main delivery: 4K where available.
- Frame rate: 24/25/30 for normal shots; 50/60 only for shots intended for slow motion.
- Hold every composition for at least 8 seconds.
- Record 10–20 seconds of clean ambient sound at each major location.
- Capture a safe wide, medium, and detail of each key subject.
- Avoid excessive digital zoom and “tourist whip-pan” movement.
- Prefer stable handheld/gimbal movement and locked shots.
- Shoot some vertical material separately; do not rely only on cropping 16:9.

## Audio to capture
- Quiet park ambience.
- Footsteps.
- Distant traffic where modern/ancient contrast is needed.
- Gate/visitor ambience.
- Wind/trees.

These sounds can make the opening feel like a real place before narration/music enters.

## People/privacy
- Prefer wide public-space compositions where individuals are incidental.
- Avoid making identifiable strangers the clear subject without permission, especially children.
- For planned close human shots, use our own companion/model or obtain consent.

## Drone
Do not build the episode around drone footage. Drone flight and filming permissions depend on the exact airspace/site/operation. Treat aerial footage as optional and verify all current flight/site permissions before any flight.

## Museum day — separate from Daereungwon shooting
If we shoot at Gyeongju National Museum:
- Museum web guidance says images on its website/eMuseum can be reused according to the specific KOGL license shown; uses outside the allowed conditions require permission.
- Museum collection reproduction/photography has separate rules. Confirm any video/commercial-production request with the museum in advance.
- Therefore, for the film, prioritize KOGL Type 1 collection images for hero artifact inserts and treat on-site museum video as a separate permission check.

## Suggested one-day sequence
1. Morning: modern street + low-crowd Daereungwon wides.
2. Late morning: Cheonmachong exterior and visitor flow.
3. Midday: signs/details/texture/backup shots.
4. Afternoon: nearby modern-city contrast footage.
5. Golden hour: hero mound wides, silhouettes, long-lens layers.
6. Twilight: final modern/ancient contrast and closing shot.

## Minimum usable package before edit
- 30+ usable modern-location video clips.
- 15+ still photos.
- 5+ clean ambience recordings.
- Cheonmachong exterior coverage.
- At least 3 strong “modern Korea + ancient mound in same frame” hero shots.

Once this package exists, the present-day side of EP01 is effectively solved; remaining visuals are licensed artifacts, archive material, original graphics, and Higgsfield historical reconstruction.
