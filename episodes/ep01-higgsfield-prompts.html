EP01 Higgsfield 프롬프트

실제 자료로 설명할 수 없는 ‘과거의 과정과 사람의 시선’을 보완하는 재현 장면 세트.

절대 원칙: 모든 역사 AI 장면에는 AI Visual Reconstruction 표기. 실제 사료에 없는 대사·정확한 감정·특정 인물의 생각을 사실처럼 만들지 않는다. AI는 증거를 대체하지 않고, 실제 발굴사진·유물·구조자료 뒤에 배치한다.

공통 비주얼 룰

시대
5세기~6세기 초 신라 왕경 경주. 정확한 연대 단정 대신 ‘early Silla elite burial context’로 표현.
톤
절제된 다큐멘터리 시네마틱. 판타지 사극·전쟁영화 느낌 금지.
의상
고대 신라계 복식에 가까운 소박한 직물과 계층 차이. 조선시대 한복, 중국 명·청 복식, 일본 사무라이 요소 금지.
건축
거대한 조선 궁궐식 기와궁전 금지. 목조건축과 작업공간을 보수적으로 표현.
카메라
느린 트래킹, 정적인 와이드, 손과 물건의 디테일. 과도한 드론·360 회전·액션캠 금지.
감정
슬픔을 연기하는 얼굴 클로즈업보다 침묵, 준비, 조직, 시선, 손의 움직임을 보여준다.
SHOT H01

목재 준비 — 무덤이 ‘공사’였다는 감각

사용 구간: 2:15–3:15

Cinematic documentary reconstruction of early Silla-period Gyeongju, Korea, around the 5th century. Skilled workers prepare thick timber beams for an elite burial chamber, measuring, carrying and fitting wood with simple period-appropriate tools. No palace spectacle, no fantasy armor, no modern objects. Focus on coordinated labor and physical effort rather than dramatic emotion. Earthy colors, cool morning light, restrained realism, natural dust in the air. Slow lateral camera movement, medium-wide composition, 35mm lens feeling, historically grounded archaeological reconstruction. No dialogue, no subtitles, no royal portrait. AI Visual Reconstruction.
SHOT H02

돌 운반 — 규모와 노동력

사용 구간: 2:15–3:15

Historically grounded early Silla burial construction scene in ancient Gyeongju. Groups of workers carry and move large river stones toward a wooden burial chamber using baskets, wooden supports and coordinated manual labor. Show repetition, weight, organization and scale. Avoid heroic posing, fantasy costume, cranes, wheeled modern carts or advanced machinery. The burial mound is only partly formed. Overcast daylight, documentary realism, slow push-in from wide shot to workers’ hands and stones, natural body movement, subdued atmosphere. AI Visual Reconstruction.
SHOT H03

부장품 준비 — ‘무엇을 넣을지 선택했다’

사용 구간: 3:15–4:20

Quiet early Silla elite funeral preparation, ancient Gyeongju. Attendants carefully arrange valuable burial goods before placement: gold ornaments inspired by verified Silla forms, glass vessels, pottery, horse equipment and personal regalia. Do not copy a modern museum display; show objects being handled as part of a living ritual process. Emphasize deliberate selection and careful placement. No invented magical symbolism, no glowing artifacts, no fantasy jewels. Soft interior daylight, close shots of hands, cloth, metal and glass, shallow depth of field, restrained documentary style. AI Visual Reconstruction.
SHOT H04

장례 준비 — 개인의 죽음보다 사회적 행위

사용 구간: 4:20–5:20

Early Silla elite burial ceremony preparation in Gyeongju, historically conservative reconstruction. Several groups occupy different roles: attendants near the burial chamber, workers managing materials, senior figures observing procedure, others carrying ritual and burial objects. The scene should communicate hierarchy and organization through spacing and behavior, not through exaggerated crowns or theatrical gestures. No dialogue, no crying closeups, no fantasy priest, no Chinese imperial court aesthetic. Static wide shot with subtle foreground movement, late afternoon light, solemn but controlled atmosphere. AI Visual Reconstruction.
SHOT H05

사고방식 투영 — ‘사람의 머릿속’이 아니라 ‘문제 상황’

사용 구간: 4:20–5:20 핵심 장면

Interpretive historical reconstruction, early Silla Gyeongju. A senior member of the community stands at a respectful distance watching a monumental burial mound being constructed while workers, attendants and valuable objects move through an organized space. Do not show an internal monologue, speaking lips, visions, ghosts or supernatural imagery. The image should suggest a social problem: an important person has died, yet rank, legitimacy and authority still need to remain visible to the living. Convey this only through composition, spatial hierarchy and controlled activity. Camera behind the observer, slow forward movement, restrained natural light, cinematic documentary realism. AI Visual Reconstruction — interpretation, not a recorded event.
SHOT H06

봉분 완성 — 권위가 ‘보이는 형태’가 되는 순간

사용 구간: 4:50–5:20 또는 클로징 플래시백

A nearly completed monumental Silla burial mound in ancient Gyeongju, earth newly packed over a stone-covered wooden chamber. Small groups of people stand at a distance, making the mound’s scale visible. The emphasis is not celebration but permanence, visibility and the transformation of labor into a landscape monument. No stone mausoleum, no pyramids, no Chinese-style tomb gateway, no modern landscaping. Low warm sunlight, long shadows, static wide shot, natural terrain, historically conservative reconstruction. AI Visual Reconstruction.
SHOT H07

과거 → 현재 매치컷

사용 구간: 6:05–7:05

Match-cut setup for a documentary transition: a newly finished Silla-era earthen burial mound in ancient Gyeongju fills the frame in a simple wide composition, camera completely stable, mound centered slightly off-axis, sparse ancient surroundings. Designed to cut directly to the same-shaped green royal tomb mound in modern Gyeongju. No people close to camera, no fantasy structures, no dramatic sky effects. Neutral daylight and realistic proportions. AI Visual Reconstruction.

Negative Prompt — 공통 금지요소

Joseon dynasty hanbok, gat hats, Ming or Qing court robes, samurai armor, European medieval armor, fantasy armor, giant stone palace, Chinese imperial palace, pagoda skyline, torii gates, modern tools, excavators, cranes, concrete, electric lights, plastic, modern roads, supernatural ghosts, glowing magic, fantasy crown, exaggerated crying, battle scene, cinematic war spectacle, modern museum display cases, text, watermark.

편집 배치 원칙

실제 자료 → AI 순서를 고정한다. 예: 1973 발굴사진 → 구조 그래픽 → H01/H02 재현. 금관·허리띠 실제 이미지 → H03 재현. 이렇게 하면 관객이 무엇이 사실 자료이고 무엇이 해석적 재현인지 자연스럽게 이해한다.

AI 장면 한 컷 길이: 3~6초 권장. 한 장면을 길게 끌기보다 실제 자료 사이의 ‘시간적 연결’에 쓴다.
